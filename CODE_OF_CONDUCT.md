# Code of conduct

MoonCache contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful,
technical, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence and reproducible cases.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect private data, credentials, and embargoed security information.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully while using documented appeal paths.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate misinformation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Exposing unpatched cache-confusion or data-disclosure details against the
  process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban
participants when behavior threatens the project or its community. Reports
should use the repository's private maintainer contact or security-advisory
channel when available.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
